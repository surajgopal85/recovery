Gentle Path — Product Thesis, v1
Gentle Path helps people in recovery turn shame, fear, resentment, dishonesty, craving, and avoidance into honest, concrete action.
The product is built on a simple belief:
Recovery is not primarily about feeling better. It is about living differently.
When someone is overwhelmed by something they are carrying, Gentle Path helps them slow it down, tell the truth about it, identify what is within their control, and choose the next useful action.
The app does not replace sponsors, meetings, fellowship, therapy, professional treatment, or the 12 Steps.
Its role is narrower:
Help the user recognize the next right action and follow through on it.

Core loop
Notice → Tell the truth → Take responsibility → Act → Connect → Release
Core unit of value
A journal entry is not the outcome.
A completed recovery-aligned action is.
Examples:
telling someone the truth
calling someone in recovery
attending a meeting
asking for help
writing an honest inventory
removing access to something harmful
making a repair
confronting something being avoided
practicing acceptance where action is impossible
Product principles
Action over soothing.
The app should never help someone feel better instead of doing the thing they need to do.
Compassion without indulgence.
Warm, nonjudgmental, and hopeful — but never saccharine or congratulatory by default.
Responsibility without shame.
Help users distinguish what happened to them from what is within their control now.
One action at a time.
Large moral or emotional problems should be reduced to manageable next steps.
Connection over isolation.
When appropriate, move the user toward trustworthy human support rather than deeper dependence on the app.
Recovery is bigger than abstinence.
Sobriety milestones matter, but the deeper goal is becoming capable of living honestly, usefully, and freely.
Emotional promise
A life you don't need to escape from.
