##Gentle Path — MVP
MVP hypothesis
Someone experiencing recovery-related fear, shame, craving, resentment, dishonesty, or avoidance will find value in a short guided process that transforms emotional confusion into one concrete recovery action.
We do not need to prove the entire long-term product.
We need to prove one thing:
Can Gentle Path take something painful that a user is carrying and help them identify and commit to a useful next action?

MVP experience
The user:
Enters the app.
Identifies why they are here.
Identifies what they are struggling with.
Describes what the struggle currently looks like.
Identifies what recovery would give them back.
Brings forward one thing they are carrying today.
Completes a short guided inventory.
Identifies or receives help identifying the next honest action.
Commits to doing it within a defined time.
Returns later to mark the action complete or reconsider it.

Success criterion
The first vertical slice succeeds when a user can move from:
“Something is wrong and I don't know what to do.”

to:
“This is the specific thing I am going to do next.”

in approximately five minutes.
Primary product metric
Eventually:
Recovery actions committed to → recovery actions completed
Not number of journal entries.
Not time spent in app.
Not engagement for engagement's sake.
